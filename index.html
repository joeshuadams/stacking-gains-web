Stacking Gains

Track your macros in seconds — or let your AI coach do it for you.

Most tracking apps die on friction: searching a database for the same lunch you ate yesterday. Stacking Gains is built around one-tap presets, your own recent meals, and an AI estimate for the food that has no label — so logging takes seconds and you actually keep doing it.

One-tap logging

Save the meals you actually eat as presets, then log them with a single tap. Your recent foods are right there too.

AI estimates

Describe a meal or photograph it and get a macro estimate broken down by ingredient. Every number is editable before it's saved.

Modes for the season you're in

Maintain, Cut, Rest Day — your own targets, named by you. The app never decides your macros for you.

Connect your AI coach

Link an assistant that speaks MCP and it can read your day and log food for you. Your tracker becomes the dashboard; your coach does the typing.

Your data

Your log belongs to you. Delete your account from inside the app at any time and everything goes with it — immediately and permanently. Food photos are used to produce an estimate and are not kept.

Stacking Gains is a tracker, not a clinician. AI estimates are approximations, and nothing in the app is medical or nutritional advice.
